Frequently asked

Golden Visa questions, answered

What is the Greece Golden Visa program?
Greece's Golden Visa grants renewable residency permits to non-EU nationals who invest in qualifying real estate, opening visa-free travel across the Schengen Area.
What is the minimum investment required?
Minimum thresholds vary by region and property type. Netherfield's current listings start from €250,000 — speak with our team for the figure that applies to your target property.
Does the Golden Visa lead to EU citizenship?
The Golden Visa grants long-term residency, not automatic citizenship. Citizenship may become possible later through separate naturalization requirements, including residency duration and language criteria.
Can my family be included?
Yes. Spouses, dependent children, and in many cases parents of the main applicant can typically be included under one investment.
How long does the process take?
Timelines vary by case, but many applicants complete property purchase and initial residency filing within a few months of engaging our team.
